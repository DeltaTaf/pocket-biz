The product idea

A compact guide that explains culture, etiquette, legal basics, market conditions, and common mistakes before someone works, studies, negotiates, or starts a business in another country.

Culture intelligence

Communication style, hierarchy, negotiation habits, decision-making, time expectations, and trust building.

Practical business guide

Company setup basics, visas, work permits, employment rules, tax overview, risks, and industries.

Pocket Advisor

An AI-style assistant that answers scenario-based questions, like what to avoid before a Japanese supplier meeting.

Country comparison

Compare two markets before entering them. Because apparently guessing based on vibes is not a strategy, despite what half the internet thinks.

Pocket Biz scores are editorial MVP indicators for comparison, not official government or economic ratings.
